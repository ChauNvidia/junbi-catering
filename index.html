Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Every event is custom, so pricing depends on the experience, your guest count, and your location. Send us your details and we'll reply with a quote within 2 business days.

What's the minimum number of guests?

We cater events of 25 or more guests. Planning something smaller? Email seattle@junbishop.com and we'll let you know what's possible.

Where do you serve?

Add your event location to the form (city, venue, or address). We'll confirm whether we can serve it, and any travel details, in your quote.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Share your event date and we'll confirm availability in your quote.

How long does each experience last?

It depends on the experience and the size of your group. We'll confirm timing in your quote.

What do you need from our venue?

Once you tell us where your event is, we'll go over space and setup needs with you so event day runs smoothly.

Can you accommodate dietary needs or allergies?

Please tell us in the "Anything else we should know?" box on the form and we'll go over what's possible with you.

Which experience should I choose?

A Matcha Bar suits gatherings where guests mingle. A Workshop suits team building. Drop-Off suits meetings and office events. Not sure? Choose "Not sure yet" in the form and we'll recommend one.

Do you work with event planners and agencies?

Yes. Planning for a client or your company is welcome.

Can I share this with my manager or team?

Yes. Each experience has a one-page PDF you can download or forward.
